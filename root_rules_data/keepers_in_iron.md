# Chapter 15: Keepers in Iron

## 15.1 Overview
The Keepers in Iron are an elite, religious order of badgers dedicated to delving for lost Relics (Tablets, Jewels, and Figures) scattered across the Woodland, encamping Waystations, and recovering artifacts to score points.

## 15.2 Special Abilities
* **15.2.1 Devout Knights:** Keeper warriors are heavily armored. When taking hits in battle, Keepers can ignore hits by spending matching Retinue cards or utilizing their armor abilities.
* **15.2.2 Relic Delving:** Keepers do not build standard buildings; instead, they place Waystation tokens (Caravan, Shrine, Forge) to dig up and recover Relic tokens from forests and clearings.
* **15.2.3 Encampment:** Keepers can convert warriors into Waystations and vice versa to establish operational bases.

## 15.3 Faction Setup
* **15.3.1 Step 1:** Place 3 Waystations (1 of each suit/type: Caravan, Shrine, Forge) on your faction board.
* **15.3.2 Step 2:** Randomly scatter Relic tokens (Tablets, Jewels, Figures) face down into the forest spaces on the map according to setup rules.
* **15.3.3 Step 3:** Choose any non-corner clearing with no enemy pieces. Place 3 Keeper warriors and 1 Waystation there.
* **15.3.4 Step 4:** Fill your Retinue columns (Move, Battle, Delve, Recover) with your starting cards as indicated on the board.

## 15.4 Turn Sequence

### 15.4.1 Birdsong
1. **Encamp:** You may swap a warrior on the map with a Waystation from your board, or vice versa, in a clearing you rule.
2. **Decrypt Retinue:** You may add up to 2 cards from your hand into your Retinue columns (Move, Battle, Delve, Recover).

### 15.4.2 Daylight
Execute actions in your Retinue columns from left to right. For each card in a column, you may perform the matching action once:
* **Move:** Move warriors from a clearing matching the card's suit.
* **Battle:** Initiate a battle in a clearing matching the card's suit.
* **Delve:** Move 1 Relic token from an adjacent forest into a clearing you rule that contains a Waystation matching the Relic's type.
* **Recover:** Score a Relic token in a clearing with a matching Waystation. To recover a Relic, you must rule all clearings surrounding the current clearing. Score victory points according to the Relic's value on your track, then return the Relic to your board.

### 15.4.3 Evening
1. **Retinue Check:** If you used cards in your Retinue that did not match the suit of the clearing where the action occurred, you must discard those cards (Fervor Loss).
2. **Draw:** Draw 1 card, plus 1 extra card per uncovered card draw icon on your Waystation tracks.
3. **Discard:** Discard down to 5 cards in hand.

# Faction Rules: Riverfolk Company

## 11. Riverfolk Company Overview
* **11.1 Commercial Interest:** The Riverfolk Company establishes trade posts along the river to score victory points. Other factions purchase their unique services using their own warriors as currency. The Riverfolk can also score points through raw accumulation of funds, but a massive treasury makes them a target.

## 11.2 Riverfolk Company Faction Rules and Abilities
* **11.2.1 Riverfolk Crafting:** The Riverfolk craft during Daylight by committing funds to empty spaces on the Trade Posts tracks. They have no crafting pieces.
* **11.2.2 Swimmers:** The Riverfolk treat rivers as paths and may move along them fully linking an origin and destination clearing, completely ignoring the need for rule.
* **11.2.3 Public Hand:** The Riverfolk place their hand entirely face up above their faction board for all players to see.
* **11.2.4 Funds:** To take many actions, the Riverfolk commit and spend FUNDS, which are warriors sitting in their Funds box.
* **11.2.5 Trade Post Disruption:** Whenever a trade post is removed, the Riverfolk must remove half of their funds, rounded up, and remove that trade post from the game permanently.
* **11.2.6 Buying Services:** At the start of another player's Birdsong, that buyer may purchase Riverfolk services. The buyer places warriors from their supply into the Riverfolk's Payments box equal to the listed cost. Each turn, a player may buy one service, plus one per clearing containing a trade post and their own faction pieces.
    * **Vagabond Payments:** The Vagabond pays for services by exhausting items; for each item exhausted to do so, the Riverfolk place one Riverfolk warrior in the Payments box.

## 11.2.7 Riverfolk Services Offered
* **11.2.7.I Hand Card:** The buyer takes any card from the Riverfolk's open hand and adds it to their own hand.
* **11.2.7.II Riverboats:** The buyer treats rivers as paths until the end of their turn.
* **11.2.7.III Mercenaries:** During Daylight and Evening of this turn (except when battling the Riverfolk), the buyer treats Riverfolk warriors as their own for rule and for battle. The buyer cannot move them, count them toward dominance, or remove them except by taking hits.
    * **Taking Hits (11.2.7.III.a):** The buyer must split hits, taking odd hits by removing owned (not Riverfolk) warriors first.
    * **Vagabond Restriction (11.2.7.III.b):** The Vagabond cannot buy Riverfolk Mercenaries. If Riverfolk Mercenaries are removed in a battle against the Vagabond, the Vagabond does not become Hostile.

## 11.4 Riverfolk Company Turn Structure: Birdsong
* **11.4.1 Protectionism:** If the Payments box is empty, place two Riverfolk warriors in it immediately.
* **11.4.2 Score Dividends:** If there are any trade posts on the map, score one victory point per two funds. Do not score for warriors currently in Payments or Committed.
* **11.4.3 Gather Funds:** Move all warriors on your faction board back to the Funds box.

## 11.5 Riverfolk Company Turn Structure: Daylight
* **11.5.0 Execution:** Commit and spend funds to take actions in any order and number. When committing a fund, move the warrior to the Committed box. When spending a fund, return the warrior to its original owner's supply.
* **11.5.1 Move:** Commit one fund to take a move.
* **11.5.2 Battle:** Commit one fund to initiate a battle.
* **11.5.3 Craft:** Commit funds to craft a card. Instead of the Committed box, place these warriors on empty spaces of the Trade Posts tracks matching the crafting cost suits.
    * **Export (11.5.3.I):** You may ignore the listed benefit of a crafted card, discarding it instead to place one Riverfolk warrior directly into the Payments box.
* **11.5.4 Draw:** Commit one fund to draw a card.
* **11.5.5 Recruit:** Spend one fund to place a warrior in any clearing with a river.
* **11.5.6 Establish Trade Post with Garrison:** Spend two funds belonging to the player who rules the target clearing to place a trade post and a Riverfolk warrior there. The clearing must not already have a trade post. Score the victory points listed on the uncovered space.

## 11.6 Riverfolk Company Turn Structure: Evening
* **11.6.1 Discard Cards:** If you have more than five cards in your hand, discard down to five.
* **11.6.2 Set Costs:** Move each service marker to any space on its track, setting a new cost for other players.

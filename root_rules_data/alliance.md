# Faction Rules: Woodland Alliance

## 8. Woodland Alliance Overview
* [cite_start]**8.1 Guerrilla Strategy:** The Alliance scores victory points by placing sympathy tokens[cite: 331]. [cite_start]They use a Supporters stack to fuel revolts, establish bases, and train officers[cite: 333, 334, 335].

## 8.2 Woodland Alliance Faction Rules and Abilities
* [cite_start]**8.2.1 Alliance Crafting:** The Alliance crafts during Daylight by activating sympathy tokens[cite: 337].
* [cite_start]**8.2.2 Alliance Ability - Guerrilla War:** As defender in battle, the Alliance deals hits equal to the higher roll, and the attacker deals hits equal to the lower roll[cite: 338].
* [cite_start]**8.2.3 The Supporters Stack:** Actions are funded by spending cards from the face-down Supporters stack[cite: 339]. [cite_start]Cards are spent only for suit and don't count against hand size[cite: 340].
    * **I. [cite_start]Capacity:** If the Alliance has no bases on the map, the stack holds up to 5 cards max; excess is discarded[cite: 342, 343]. [cite_start]Stack capacity becomes unlimited once a base is on the map[cite: 344].
* [cite_start]**8.2.4 Removing Bases:** When a base is removed, discard all supporters matching that clearing's suit (including birds) and remove half of your officers, rounded up[cite: 345]. [cite_start]If no bases remain, discard down to 5 supporters[cite: 346].
* [cite_start]**8.2.6 Alliance Ability - Outrage:** Whenever another player removes a sympathy token or moves any warriors into a sympathetic clearing, they must add 1 card matching that clearing's suit from their hand to the Supporters stack[cite: 350]. [cite_start]If they have no matching cards, they must show their hand, and the Alliance draws a card from the deck to add to the stack[cite: 351].

## 8.4 Woodland Alliance Turn Structure: Birdsong
* [cite_start]**8.4.1 Alliance Action - Revolt:** Choose a sympathetic clearing without a base matching a base on your board[cite: 362]. [cite_start]Spend two supporters matching its suit[cite: 363]. [cite_start]Remove all enemy pieces from that clearing[cite: 364]. [cite_start]Place the matching base, place warriors equal to the matching sympathetic clearings, and place 1 warrior in the Officers box[cite: 365, 366].
* [cite_start]**8.4.2 Alliance Action - Spread Sympathy:** Choose an unsympathetic clearing adjacent to a sympathetic one (or any clearing if none exist)[cite: 369, 370]. [cite_start]Spend matching supporters listed on the track[cite: 371, 372].
    * [cite_start]**Martial Law:** You must spend an extra matching supporter if the target clearing has $\ge 3$ warriors of another player[cite: 373].

## 8.5 Woodland Alliance Turn Structure: Daylight
* **8.5.1 Craft:** Activate sympathy tokens to craft cards[cite: 378].
* [cite_start]**8.5.2 Mobilize:** Add a card from your hand to the Supporters stack[cite: 379].
* [cite_start]**8.5.3 Train:** Spend a card whose suit matches a base clearing on the map to place a warrior in the Officers box to become an officer[cite: 380, 381].

## 8.6 Woodland Alliance Turn Structure: Evening
* **8.6.1 Military Operations:** Take actions up to your total number of officers, in any order[cite: 384]:
    * **I. [cite_start]Move:** Take one move[cite: 386].
    * **II. [cite_start]Battle:** Initiate a battle[cite: 387].
    * **III. [cite_start]Recruit:** Place a warrior in a clearing with an active base[cite: 388].
    * **IV. [cite_start]Organize:** Remove an Alliance warrior from an unsympathetic clearing to place a sympathy token there, then score the uncovered points[cite: 389].

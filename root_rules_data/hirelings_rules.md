# Appendix H: Hireling Rules & Mechanics

## H.1 Overview & Incompatibility
Hirelings are neutral forces that players compete to control for 1–3 turns before their allegiance shifts to another player. 
* **3-Hireling Limit:** Exactly 3 hirelings are used in any game of Root.
* **Faction Incompatibility:** A hireling cannot be used in a game containing its parent faction (e.g., you CANNOT use the Forest Patrol hireling if the Marquise de Cat is a playing faction).

## H.2 Promoted vs. Demoted Sides
* **Promoted Side (3–4 Players):** Uses physical wooden pieces on the map. The controlling player moves and battles with them.
* **Demoted Side (5+ Players):** The hireling provides a passive ability or stat boost to its controller instead of placing pieces on the board.

## H.3 Gaining and Passing Control
1. **Uncontrolled Pool:** All 3 hirelings start uncontrolled in the supply.
2. **Score Threshold Markers:** Hireling markers are placed on spaces 4, 8, and 12 of the score track. The *first* player to reach or pass a marker claims it.
3. **Drafting (End of Turn):** At the end of your turn, if you hold a hireling marker, take a hireling card from the supply, roll the **Control Die**, and place control markers on the hireling equal to the roll.
   * *Catch-Up Die Rule:* If you have the most victory points (or are tied), count ONLY gold pips on the die. If you are behind, count ALL pips (gold + red).
4. **Relinquishing Control:** At the end of your turn, remove 1 control marker from each hireling you control. Once the last control marker is removed, you MUST pass that hireling card to the player with the fewest victory points (you cannot keep it).

## H.4 Rule & Combat Interactions
* **Rule Contributions:** Promoted Hireling pieces DO count toward ruling a clearing for their current controller.
* **Not Enemies:** Hirelings are NOT enemy pieces to their current controller. They CANNOT be attacked or targeted by their controller's actions.
* **Opponent Combat:** Enemies can attack Hireling pieces normally. When defending, Hireling pieces deal rolled hits back using the current controller's status.

# Hireling Edge Cases & FAQ Pairs

## FAQ 1: Hirelings and Dominance Cards
### Question: Can a player count clearings ruled solely by Hireling pieces to fulfill a Dominance Card victory condition?
* **Community Consensus:** Yes.
* **Explanation:** While you control a Hireling, its pieces are treated as friendly pieces for the purpose of checking Rule (Rule 2.5.1). If you rule the required suit clearings using a combination of your warriors and controlled Hireling pieces, you satisfy the Dominance requirement (Rule 3.3.1).
* **Source Reference:** Law of Root Appendix H & Rule 2.5.1

## FAQ 2: Infamy and Hireling Pieces
### Question: Does the Vagabond score Infamy (+1 VP) when removing Hireling pieces in battle?
* **Community Consensus:** Yes, provided the Hireling is controlled by an Hostile opponent.
* **Explanation:** If an opponent controls a Hireling, those pieces are treated as enemy pieces belonging to that player during battle. Removing them in battle on your turn triggers standard Infamy scoring (Rule 9.2.2.III). Uncontrolled Hirelings do not belong to an opponent and do not yield Infamy points.
* **Source Reference:** Law of Root 9.2.2.III & Appendix H

## FAQ 3: Outrage and Moving Hirelings
### Question: If I move a controlled Hireling into a clearing containing Woodland Alliance sympathy, who pays Outrage?
* **Community Consensus:** The player controlling the Hireling pays Outrage from their own hand.
* **Explanation:** Rule 8.2.6 (Outrage) triggers whenever "another player moves any pieces into a sympathetic clearing." Because you are executing the action to move the Hireling during your turn, you are responsible for matching cards from your hand.
* **Source Reference:** Law of Root 8.2.6 & Appendix H

## FAQ 4: Field Hospitals and Hireling Casualties
### Question: Can the Marquise de Cat use Field Hospitals to save Hireling warriors that are removed in battle?
* **Community Consensus:** No.
* **Explanation:** Rule 6.2.3 (Field Hospitals) explicitly applies only to "Marquise warriors." Hireling pieces are distinct faction pieces and are returned to the Hireling card pool or map reserve, never to the Marquise's Keep.
* **Source Reference:** Law of Root 6.2.3 & Appendix H

## FAQ 5: Field Placement Restrictions (The Keep)
### Question: Can a player move or place Hirelings into the clearing containing the Marquise de Cat's Keep?
* **Community Consensus:** They can MOVE Hirelings into the Keep clearing, but CANNOT PLACE Hirelings there.
* **Explanation:** Rule 6.2.2 (The Keep) dictates that enemy players cannot *place* pieces in the Keep clearing, but *movement* into the clearing is permitted. Any Hireling ability that specifies "placing" a piece in a clearing is blocked by the Keep, but standard "Move" actions are allowed.
* **Source Reference:** Law of Root 6.2.2 & Appendix H

# Faction Rules: Vagabond & Knaves of the Deepwood

## 9. Vagabond Overview & Abilities
* [cite_start]**9.2.1 Vagabond Crafting:** The Vagabond exhausts items to activate its pawn as a crafting piece, ignoring once-per-turn limits[cite: 398].
* [cite_start]**9.2.2 Lone Wanderer:** The Vagabond pawn is not a warrior (cannot rule clearings or prevent rule)[cite: 400]. [cite_start]The pawn cannot be removed from the map[cite: 401].
    * **I. [cite_start]Full Removal Effects:** Effects that remove all enemy pieces (e.g., revolts, bombs) force the Vagabond to damage three items instead of removal[cite: 402].
* **9.2.3 Nimble:** Can move regardless of who rules the origin or destination clearing[cite: 403].
* [cite_start]**9.2.6 Maximum Rolled Hits:** In battle, the Vagabond's maximum rolled hits equals the number of undamaged items in his Satchel[cite: 414].
* [cite_start]**9.2.7 Taking Hits:** For each hit taken, the Vagabond must damage one undamaged item, moving it to the Damaged box[cite: 415]. [cite_start]If no undamaged items remain, ignore remaining hits[cite: 416].
* [cite_start]**9.2.8 Coalitions:** Cannot activate dominance cards for normal victory conditions[cite: 417]. [cite_start]In 4+ player games, can use dominance to form a coalition with the player with the fewest points[cite: 418, 419]. [cite_start]If that player wins, the Vagabond wins[cite: 421].

## 9.2.9 Relationships Track & Infamy
* **9.2.9.I Improving Relationships:** Advance relationship markers by completing the Aid action a number of times during the same turn[cite: 424, 426].
* [cite_start]**9.2.9.II Allied Status:** Reaching the final space makes you ALLIED[cite: 430]. [cite_start]You can move Allied warriors with your pawn and treat them as your own when attacking[cite: 434, 436].
    * **Taking Hits with Allies:** You can remove Allied warriors to take hits[cite: 439]. If you remove more Allied warriors than you damage items in a battle, that faction becomes Hostile[cite: 440].
* [cite_start]**9.2.9.III Hostile Status:** Removing a warrior of a non-Hostile faction slides them to the Hostile box[cite: 442].
    * [cite_start]**Infamy (9.2.9.III.a):** Score one extra victory point for each piece of a Hostile faction you remove in battle during your turn[cite: 444].
    * **Moving into Hostile Clearings (9.2.9.III.b):** Must exhaust an extra boot item to move into a clearing containing any Hostile warriors[cite: 446].

## 9.4 Vagabond: Birdsong Actions
* [cite_start]**9.4.1 Refresh:** Flip exhausted items face up based on your status on the Refresh track, plus three more[cite: 467, 468].
* [cite_start]**9.4.2 Slip:** Move into an adjacent clearing or forest without exhausting items, ignoring all movement restrictions[cite: 469, 470].

## 9.5 Vagabond: Daylight Actions
Exhaust items to take actions:
* **9.5.1 Move:** Exhaust 1 boot to move[cite: 474].
* [cite_start]**9.5.2 Battle:** Exhaust 1 sword to initiate battle[cite: 476].
* [cite_start]**9.5.3 Explore:** Exhaust 1 torch to take an item from under a ruin in your clearing, scoring 1 point[cite: 477, 478].
* **9.5.4 Aid:** Exhaust any item and give a matching card from your hand to a player in your clearing to take an item from their Crafted Items box[cite: 479, 480].
* [cite_start]**9.5.5 Quest:** Match clearing suit, exhaust the two items listed on a quest card to complete it, scoring points or drawing cards[cite: 482, 484].
* [cite_start]**9.5.6 Strike:** Exhaust 1 crossbow to remove an enemy warrior from your clearing[cite: 486].

## 18. Knaves of the Deepwood (Homeland Expansion)
* **18.2.1 Exclusive with Vagabond:** The Knaves of the Deepwood cannot be played in the same game as the Vagabond[cite: 1002].
* [cite_start]**18.2.4 Deepwood Runners:** Captains and Skunks are warriors that can move in and out of forests, completely ignoring rule requirements[cite: 1008].
* [cite_start]**18.2.5 Follow Me:** When moving a Captain, you may move Skunks along with them[cite: 1009]. [cite_start]Captains cannot be hit in battle unless all Skunks in the clearing have been removed first[cite: 1010].

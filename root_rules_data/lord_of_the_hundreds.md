# Chapter 14: Lord of the Hundreds

## 14.1 Overview
The Lord of the Hundreds is an aggressive, mob-driven faction focused on ruling clearings, spreading Mob tokens, and burning down enemy buildings while hoarding Relics and items on their Mob track.

## 14.2 Special Abilities
* **14.2.1 Contempt for the Unruly:** The Lord of the Hundreds scores victory points based on how many clearings they rule that contain Mob tokens, as well as by destroying enemy buildings and tokens with Mobs.
* **14.2.2 The Warlord:** The Lord of the Hundreds has a unique leader piece called the Warlord. The Warlord is a warrior piece that also counts as a leader. If the Warlord is ever removed from the map, place it on the Warlord box on the faction board; it will be placed back onto the map during the next Birdsong.
* **14.2.3 Moods:** The Lord of the Hundreds chooses a Mood card each turn during Birdsong. The selected Mood modifies the Warlord's capabilities and action limits for that turn.

## 14.3 Faction Setup
* **14.3.1 Step 1:** Choose a starting corner clearing opposite to the Marquise de Cat's Keep (if present), or any corner clearing if the Marquise is not playing.
* **14.3.2 Step 2:** Place the Warlord, 4 warriors, and 1 Mob token in your starting clearing.
* **14.3.3 Step 3:** Place 1 Mob token in each clearing adjacent to your starting clearing.
* **14.3.4 Step 4:** Fill your Mob track with your Mob tokens. Place your Mood cards nearby.

## 14.4 Turn Sequence

### 14.4.1 Birdsong
1. **Prowl:** If the Warlord is on your faction board, place it in any clearing with any of your warriors or Mob tokens.
2. **Choose Mood:** Select 1 Mood card from your Mood deck and place it active on your board, discarding the previous turn's Mood.
3. **Anoint:** You may craft using items from your hoard or cards from your hand.

### 14.4.2 Daylight
During Daylight, you may perform actions matching your active Mood and your available command stats. You spend items from your hoard to execute actions:
* **Advance:** Move the Warlord and any number of warriors from its clearing, then you may initiate 1 battle in the destination clearing.
* **Recruit:** Place warriors in clearings containing Mob tokens.
* **Raze:** Remove 1 Mob token from a clearing to remove ALL enemy buildings and tokens in that clearing. Score 1 VP for each building and token removed this way.
* **Build Mob:** Place a Mob token in an adjacent clearing that does not already contain a Mob token.

### 14.4.3 Evening
1. **Opulence:** Score victory points based on the total number of clearings you rule that contain at least 1 Mob token, according to the Mob track on your board.
2. **Restock:** Draw 1 card, plus 1 additional card per bonus draw icon uncovered on your board.
3. **Discard:** Discard down to 5 cards in hand.

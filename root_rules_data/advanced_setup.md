# Appendix A: Advanced Setup (AdSet)

## A.1 Universal Setup Sequence
Advanced Setup replaces the standard faction setup steps for all players.

1. **Map & Deck Selection:** Choose a map and deck (Standard or Exiles & Partisans).
2. **Landmarks & Clearing Markers:** Roll map clearings if applicable. If using Landmarks, place them according to setup rules.
3. **Deal Hand:** Deal 5 cards to every player. Each player will discard down to 3 cards after picking their faction.
4. **Form the Faction Pool:** Randomly draw faction setup cards equal to the number of players + 1. Place them face up.
5. **Setup Reach / Priority Check:** Ensure the draft pool contains enough Reach (or valid faction combinations) for the player count.

## A.2 Draft & Seat Order Execution
* **Draft Order:** Draft in REVERSE turn order (Player 4 picks first, then Player 3, Player 2, and finally Player 1).
* **Draft Action:** When choosing a faction card from the pool:
  1. Take the faction card and its physical game pieces.
  2. Follow the **Advanced Setup instructions on the back of that specific faction card** immediately.
  3. Discard 2 cards from your initial 5-card hand, leaving you with 3 starting cards.
* **First Player:** Player 1 (who drafted last) takes the first turn of the game.

# Faction-Specific Advanced Setup Rules

## Marquise de Cat
* **Step 1:** Choose 3 homeland clearings, each adjacent to one another.
* **Step 2:** Put 2 warriors in each of your homelands. Put 1 warrior in each other clearing.
* **Step 3:** Put the keep token in one of your homelands, not adjacent to an enemy homeland if able. Put 1 sawmill, workshop, and recruiter on the map, each in a different homeland of yours.
* **Step 4:** Fill your Buildings track with your buildings, except the leftmost spaces.

## Eyrie Dynasties
* **Step 1:** Choose a homeland clearing on the map edge that has 2+ clearings between it and enemy homelands.
* **Step 2:** Put 6 warriors and 1 roost in your homeland.
* **Step 3:** Put any leader card on your Leader Card slot. Keep the other 3 leaders face up nearby.
* **Step 4:** Tuck your 2 Loyal Viziers under the Decree column slots as listed on your current leader.
* **Step 5:** Fill your Roosts spaces with roosts, except the leftmost.

## Woodland Alliance
* **Step 1:** Draw 3 cards and add them face down to your Supporters stack.
* **Step 2:** Fill your Sympathy track with sympathy tokens.
* **Step 3:** Put your 3 bases on your matching Bases spaces.

## Vagabond
* **Step 1:** Put your pawn in any forest.
* **Step 2:** Shuffle the quest deck. Draw 3 quests to deal out nearby.
* **Step 3:** Put the 4 "R" items under the ruins randomly, unless a non-Vagabond has done so.
* **Step 4:** Put your assigned character card in your Character Card slot. Put the listed "S" items in your Satchel and on their matching tracks, as needed.
* **Step 5:** Put relationship markers for non-Vagabond factions in play on the Indifferent space of Relationships (after setup).

## Riverfolk Company
* **Step 1:** Put 4 warriors among any clearings along the river.
* **Step 2:** Put 3 warriors in your Payments box.
* **Step 3:** Fill your Trade Posts tracks with matching trade posts.
* **Step 4:** Put your 3 service markers on your Services track, setting a price for each service.

## Lizard Cult
* **Step 1:** Choose a homeland clearing that is not adjacent to enemy homelands.
* **Step 2:** Put 4 warriors and 1 matching garden in your homeland. Put 3 warriors in clearings adjacent to it as evenly as possible.
* **Step 3:** Put 2 warriors in your Acolytes box.
* **Step 4:** Fill your Gardens tracks with gardens, except the leftmost space of the garden on the map.
* **Step 5:** Put your Outcast marker on its Outcast side on any space of the Outcast box.

## Underground Duchy
* **Step 1:** Choose a homeland clearing that is not adjacent to enemy homelands.
* **Step 2:** Put 2 warriors and 1 tunnel in your homeland. Put 5 warriors among clearings adjacent to it as evenly as possible.
* **Step 3:** Put your Burrow board near the map. Fill your Buildings tracks with your citadels and markets. Put your 9 minister cards face up on your Unswayed Ministers pile. Put your 9 crowns on the square spaces showing victory points.

## Corvid Conspiracy
* **Step 1:** Choose a homeland clearing. Put 1 warrior and 1 plot token of your choice face down there.
* **Step 2:** Put 1 warrior in a clearing of each suit. (Counting the previous step, place 4 warriors in total.)

## Lord of the Hundreds
* **Step 1:** Choose a homeland clearing on the map edge that has 2+ clearings between it and enemy homelands.
* **Step 2:** Put your warlord, 4 warriors, and 1 stronghold in your homeland.
* **Step 3:** Put your Stubborn mood card in your Mood Card slot.
* **Step 4:** Put the 4 "R" items under the ruins randomly, unless this has already been done.

## Keepers in Iron
* **Step 1:** Shuffle all 12 relic tokens face down. Put one face down randomly in each forest.
* **Step 2:** Choose 2 adjacent homeland clearings on the map edge that have 2+ clearings between them and enemy homelands. Put 4 warriors in each homeland.
* **Step 3:** Put any remaining relics, as evenly as possible, among any forests that are not adjacent to your homelands.
* **Step 4:** Tuck a Faithful Retainer card into each of your Retinue column slots.

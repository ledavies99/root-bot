# Faction Rules: Marquise de Cat

## 6. Marquise de Cat Overview
* [cite_start]**6.1 Economy:** The Marquise scores victory points by constructing workshops, sawmills, and recruiters[cite: 234]. [cite_start]She must maintain and protect a strong, interconnected economy of wood to fuel construction[cite: 236].

## 6.2 Marquise Faction Rules and Abilities
* [cite_start]**6.2.1 Marquise Crafting:** The Marquise crafts during Daylight by activating workshops[cite: 238].
* [cite_start]**6.2.2 Marquise Ability - The Keep:** The Marquise can place pieces in the clearing with the keep token, and other players cannot[cite: 239]. [cite_start]Pieces may be moved into it by enemies, but pieces cannot be placed there[cite: 240]. [cite_start]If the keep is removed, it cannot be replaced[cite: 240].
* [cite_start]**6.2.3 Marquise Ability - Field Hospitals:** Whenever any number of Marquise warriors are removed from a clearing, the Marquise may spend a card matching that clearing to place those warriors in the keep clearing instead of returning them to the supply[cite: 241].

## 6.3 Marquise Faction Setup
* [cite_start]**6.3.1 Step 1:** Form supplies of 25 warriors and 8 wood tokens[cite: 243].
* [cite_start]**6.3.2 Step 2:** Place the keep token in any corner clearing[cite: 244].
* [cite_start]**6.3.3 Step 3:** Place a warrior in each clearing except the corner diagonally opposite from the keep[cite: 245].
* [cite_start]**6.3.4 Step 4:** Place 1 sawmill, 1 workshop, and 1 recruiter among the keep clearing and adjacent clearings[cite: 246, 247].
* [cite_start]**6.3.5 Step 5:** Fill tracks with remaining buildings from right to left[cite: 250].

## 6.4 Marquise Turn Structure: Birdsong
* [cite_start]**6.4.1 Wood Generation:** Place wood tokens in each clearing with any sawmills, one wood per sawmill there[cite: 253].

## 6.5 Marquise Turn Structure: Daylight
* [cite_start]**6.5.0 Execution Order:** First, activate workshops to craft cards[cite: 255]. [cite_start]Then, take up to three actions (plus one extra action per bird card spent)[cite: 256].
* [cite_start]**6.5.1 Marquise Daylight Action - Battle:** Initiate a battle[cite: 257].
* [cite_start]**6.5.2 Marquise Daylight Action - March:** Take up to two moves[cite: 258].
* [cite_start]**6.5.3 Marquise Daylight Action - Recruit:** Place one warrior at each recruiter building[cite: 259]. [cite_start]You may take this action only once per turn[cite: 259].
* [cite_start]**6.5.4 Marquise Daylight Action - Build:** Place a building on a slot in a clearing you rule[cite: 260, 261]. [cite_start]Take the leftmost building of its type from your board[cite: 262].
    * [cite_start]**Paying Cost (6.5.4.II):** Remove wood tokens equal to the cost from the chosen clearing, any adjacent ruled clearings, or clearings connected to the destination through a continuous path of clearings you rule[cite: 265]. [cite_start]Score the uncovered points[cite: 266].
* [cite_start]**6.5.5 Marquise Daylight Action - Overwork:** Spend a card matching the clearing of a sawmill to place a wood token there[cite: 267].

## 6.6 Marquise Turn Structure: Evening
* [cite_start]**6.6.1 Draw & Discard:** Draw 1 card plus uncovered bonuses[cite: 268]. [cite_start]Discard down to 5 cards if hand size exceeds 5[cite: 269].

# Faction Rules: Lizard Cult

## 10. Lizard Cult Overview
* **10.1 Radicalization:** The Lizard Cult seeks to overwhelm its foes through sheer force of will. Wherever the Cult rules clearings, it can build GARDENS to spread its gospel. The Cult acts chiefly by revealing cards to perform RITUALS, returning them to their hand in Evening. Movement and battle require radicalized ACOLYTES, gained as Cult warriors are sacrificed or defeated while defending.

## 10.2 Lizard Cult Faction Rules and Abilities
* **10.2.1 Lizard Cult Crafting:** The Cult crafts during Evening by activating gardens whose printed suit matches the active Outcast suit. This fulfills a crafting icon matching the garden's clearing, regardless of its printed suit.
* **10.2.2 Hatred of Birds:** Bird cards are not wild for Cult rituals.
* **10.2.3 Revenge:** Whenever a Cult warrior is removed while defending in battle, it is placed in the Acolytes box instead of the Cult's supply.
* **10.2.4 Pilgrims:** The Cult rules any clearings where they have any gardens. This overrides the Eyrie's Lords of the Forest ability.
* **10.2.5 Fear of the Faithful:** Whenever a garden is removed, the Cult must discard a random card.
* **10.2.6 The Lost Souls Pile:** Whenever any card is spent or discarded (even a dominance card), place it in the Lost Souls pile instead of the normal discard pile.

## 10.4 Lizard Cult Turn Structure: Birdsong
* **10.4.1 Adjust Outcast:** Look at the cards in the Lost Souls pile, ignoring birds. The suit with the most cards becomes the new Outcast. If that suit was already the Outcast, flip the marker to Hated. If no single suit has the most cards, it stays on the current suit, flipping to Hated if it wasn't already.
* **10.4.2 Discard Lost Souls:** Discard all cards in the Lost Souls pile to the main discard pile, making dominance cards available to be taken.
* **10.4.3 Perform Conspiracies:** Spend acolytes to perform conspiracies in clearings matching the Outcast suit. If the Outcast is Hated, conspiracies cost one fewer acolyte.
    * **I. Crusade:** Spend two acolytes to initiate a battle in an Outcast clearing, or move from an Outcast clearing and then optionally battle in the destination.
    * **II. Convert:** Spend two acolytes to replace an enemy warrior from an Outcast clearing with a Cult warrior.
    * **III. Sanctify:** Spend three acolytes to replace an enemy building from an Outcast clearing with a garden of the Outcast suit.

## 10.5 Lizard Cult Turn Structure: Daylight
* **10.5.0 Execution:** Reveal any number of cards from your hand into your play area to perform one ritual per card revealed. These cards cannot be used for any other purposes during Daylight.
* **10.5.1 Build:** In a clearing you rule matching the revealed card, place a garden whose printed suit matches the clearing suit.
* **10.5.2 Recruit:** In a clearing matching the revealed card, place a warrior.
* **10.5.3 Score:** Spend the same card you reveal (placing it in Lost Souls) to score victory points listed above the rightmost empty Gardens space of that suit. May perform only once per turn per suit, and cannot score if no gardens of that suit are on the map.
* **10.5.4 Sacrifice:** Place a warrior in the Acolytes box. To perform this ritual, you must reveal a bird card.

## 10.6 Lizard Cult Turn Structure: Evening
* **10.6.1 Return Revealed Cards:** Return all cards you revealed this turn to your hand.
* **10.6.2 Craft:** Craft by activating gardens whose printed suit matches the Outcast suit.
* **10.6.3 Draw and Discard:** Draw one card plus bonuses, then discard down to 5 cards if needed.

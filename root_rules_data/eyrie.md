# Faction Rules: Eyrie Dynasties

## 7. Eyrie Dynasties Overview
* [cite_start]**7.1 The Decree:** The Eyrie score points based on the number of roosts on the map during Evening[cite: 273]. [cite_start]They are bound by their Decree, a set of mandated actions[cite: 275]. [cite_start]They must take ALL actions on their Decree each turn or fall into TURMOIL[cite: 276].

## 7.2 Eyrie Faction Rules and Abilities
* [cite_start]**7.2.1 Eyrie Crafting:** The Eyrie craft before resolving the Decree during Daylight by activating roosts[cite: 278].
* [cite_start]**7.2.2 Eyrie Ability - Lords of the Forest:** The Eyrie rule a clearing when tied for most combined warriors and buildings, provided they have at least one Eyrie piece there[cite: 279].
* [cite_start]**7.2.3 Eyrie Ability - Disdain for Trade:** Whenever the Eyrie craft an item, they ignore the listed victory points and instead score only one victory point[cite: 280].

## 7.4 Eyrie Turn Structure: Birdsong
* [cite_start]**7.4.1 Emergency Orders:** If you have zero cards in your hand, draw one card[cite: 292].
* [cite_start]**7.4.2 Add to the Decree:** You must add one or two cards to the Decree from your hand[cite: 293]. [cite_start]Only one card added may be a bird card[cite: 293]. [cite_start]You can add cards to any column[cite: 294].
* [cite_start]**7.4.3 A New Roost:** If you have no roosts on the map, place a roost and 3 warriors in the clearing with the fewest warriors where all those pieces can be placed[cite: 296].

## 7.5 Eyrie Turn Structure: Daylight
* [cite_start]**7.5.1 Crafting:** You may activate roosts to craft cards from your hand[cite: 300].
* [cite_start]**7.5.2 Resolve the Decree:** You must resolve the Decree columns starting from left to right[cite: 301]. [cite_start]In each column, all cards must be resolved in any order[cite: 302]. [cite_start]If you cannot fully take an action, you immediately fall into turmoil[cite: 304].
    * **I. [cite_start]Recruit:** Place a warrior in any clearing with a roost matching the card suit[cite: 305].
    * **II. [cite_start]Move:** Move from any clearing whose suit matches the card suit (Must move at least 1 warrior)[cite: 306].
    * **III. [cite_start]Battle:** Initiate a battle in any clearing whose suit matches the card suit[cite: 307].
    * **IV. [cite_start]Build:** Place a roost in any clearing you rule whose suit matches the card suit and has no roost[cite: 308].

## 7.7 Turmoil Action Sequence
* **7.7.1 Step 1 - Humiliate:** Lose one victory point per bird card (including Loyal Viziers) on the Decree[cite: 317].
* [cite_start]**7.7.2 Step 2 - Purge:** Discard all cards on the Decree except your 2 Loyal Viziers[cite: 318].
* [cite_start]**7.7.3 Step 3 - Depose:** Flip current leader face down[cite: 319]. [cite_start]Choose a new face-up leader[cite: 319]. [cite_start]Tuck Loyal Viziers into columns listed on the new leader[cite: 320].
* **7.7.4 Step 4 - Rest:** End Daylight immediately and begin Evening[cite: 322].

## 7.8 Eyrie Leaders Reference
* [cite_start]**7.8.1 Builder:** Viziers start on Recruit and Move[cite: 324]. [cite_start]Crafting ignores Disdain for Trade[cite: 324].
* **7.8.2 Charismatic:** Viziers start on Recruit and Battle[cite: 325]. Recruit actions place two warriors instead of one[cite: 325].
* [cite_start]**7.8.3 Commander:** Viziers start on Move and Battle[cite: 326]. [cite_start]In battle as attacker, deal an extra hit[cite: 326].
* **7.8.4 Despot:** Viziers start on Move and Build[cite: 327]. Score 1 extra victory point when removing enemy buildings or tokens in battle[cite: 327].
